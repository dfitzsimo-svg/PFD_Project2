30/09/26

- Discovered that the TP4056 will only charge with a USB-A to C type charger as with  C-C, the IC requires detecting a device is plugged in using two 5.1 K Ohm resistors which the TP4056 does not have and therefore does not return a positive signal.

- Debating between a dedicated 3.3 V power rail or inputting 5V into the ESP and then distributing from there.
Will have to first perform a test to see if the GPS module can run reliably on a 3.3V input in which case Ill know everything is happy on 3.3 V and can start designing my PCB.

08/10/26

- Have begun making my schematic on Kicad, its going slightly slow at first while I learn the ins and outs of the software.

- I have decided to use an LDO in my PCB circuit to supply a fixed 3.3v to the whole board. This part will be smaller, cheaper and more efficient than the MT3068 and will not require tuning each time I make one. 

- I have decided to make dedicated custom footprints for each part Instead of just using pin headers. I believe this will make it easy place each component on the PCB, allowing for a smaller PCB in general. I also think this will lower the risk of me making any mistakes when designing the PCB.

- Instead of using pin headers, I will be soldering each component to the board itself. I think this will look much better and cuts down on parts plus assembly time.
